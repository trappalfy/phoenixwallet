Before you write

Perigee is currently an interface preview. It runs on sample data generated on your own machine: it connects to no network, derives no keys, and cannot hold, send or receive real funds. A wallet created in it starts empty and stays empty.

That rules out most of what people usually need wallet support for:

  • There are no funds to recover, and no transaction to look up. Nothing in this build ever touched a blockchain.
  • The recovery phrase it displays is sample text. It unlocks nothing, here or in any other wallet, and it is regenerated each time.
  • Nothing you type is transmitted anywhere, so there is no account on our side to look at, reset, or delete.

What is worth writing about: something in the interface is broken or confusing, a screen renders wrong in your browser, or you have a question about how the finished wallet is meant to work.

Never send us these

We will never ask for a recovery phrase, a private key, or a password — not by email, not in the extension, not anywhere. Anyone who does is trying to rob you, whichever wallet they claim to represent. Do not paste one into a reply to us either.

Privacy

What the site and the extension collect — nothing — is written out in full in the privacy policy. Questions about it go to privacy@perigeewallet.space.
